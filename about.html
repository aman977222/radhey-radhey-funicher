OUR PHILOSOPHY

Pure Solid Wood. Zero Compromise.

Radhey Radhey Furniture & Handicrafts ki shuruat ek saral sankalp ke sath hui thi — ki har bharatiya ghar me aisi lakdi ka furniture pahuche jo sadiyon tak sath nibhaye.

Aajkal market me saste chemical-mixed particle board aur fake veneer ka prachalan hai jo 2-3 saal me kharab ho jate hain. Hum keval certified 100% Solid Sheesham (Indian Rosewood) aur Teak Wood (Sagwan) ka istemal karte hain, jise vacuum-pressure chemical seasoning process se guzara jata hai taaki termite aur fungus ka 0% risk rahe.
